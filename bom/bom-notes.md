# BOM notes

ReadyKit is software. The bill of materials lists the kit's modules and free dependencies so the numbering matches the exploded view in `media/exploded.png` and drawing RDK-DWG-001 (items 1 to 7). Items 8 to 11 are not shown in the view.

Every line is priced. All costs are USD 0.00: every module is written in-house under MIT, and every dependency is free and open source (licenses in RDK-CAL-001, section F). The total, $0.00, equals `budget_usd` of $0 in `project.yaml`, so the budget is not exceeded. No new budget was recommended. Contributor time is not costed here.

The only paid option is private-repository CI time beyond GitHub's included minutes, which the tool does not need.
